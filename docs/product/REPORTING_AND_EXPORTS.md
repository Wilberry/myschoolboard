# Reporting and Exports

## Reporting model

The platform must support operational reports, class summaries, attendance reports, finance reports, and approved publication records.

## Export expectations

- Exports require explicit permissions and tenant scope checks.
- Exports must maintain record lineage and not bypass approval rules.
- Sensitive exports must be logged and access controlled.

## Report categories

- Student academic summary
- Attendance snapshots
- Result-sheet summaries
- Payment and receipting history
- Audit and operational status
- Parent access history for approved cases

## Explicit requirement

Exports are optional but must be documented and access-controlled before implementation. They must never be used to bypass approval or publication rules.
