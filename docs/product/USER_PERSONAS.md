# User Personas

## Platform Super Admin

- Oversees multi-tenant platform health.
- Manages subscriptions, tenant lifecycle, platform audits, and operational settings.
- Must not automatically access every school’s academic records.

## School Super Admin

- Owns school-level configuration and final publication of official results.
- Manages school administrators and role assignments within the tenant.
- Cannot access Platform Trash or other tenant data.

## School Administrator

- Supports school operations, enrollment, fees, user administration, and daily service tasks.
- Operates within scoped permissions.

## HM / Head of Primary

- Oversees the primary section within defined scope.
- Approves primary class attendance, lesson notes, result sheets, and relevant academic workflows.

## Principal

- Oversees the secondary section.
- Creates and manages secondary classes and assignments within authority.

## Form Teacher

- Coordinates a class and monitors students within the assigned class.
- Submits attendance and coordinates class-level academic duty records.

## Subject Teacher

- Teaches a subject or subject set and submits scores or content as allowed by authorized scope.
- Must not self-assign classes or subjects.

## Parent / Guardian

- Views child data and school communication within explicit authorization.
- Must only access children linked within the same tenant.

## Student

- Accesses permitted assignments, assessments, learning resources, and official report card data according to policy.
- Must not access other students’ private information or unpublished reports.

## Support or operations user

- A platform support role if approved.
- Must operate under restricted scope, reason logging, and audit requirements.
