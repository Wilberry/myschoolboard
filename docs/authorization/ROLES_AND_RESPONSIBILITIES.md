# Roles and Responsibilities

## Platform roles

### Platform Super Admin

- Manages tenant lifecycle, platform configuration, subscriptions, plans, billing, platform health, and platform Trash.
- Must not automatically access school academic records.

### Platform support / operations user

- Restrictive support role if approved.
- Must have explicit reasons, limited scope, and audit logging.

## School roles

### School Super Admin

- Owns school configuration and school administrator management.
- Performs final publication approval for official report cards.
- Does not edit academic records merely by virtue of the role.

### School Administrator

- Performs school operational tasks within scope.
- Manages user permissions and administrative functions under the school policy.

### HM / Head of Primary

- Oversees the primary section.
- Reviews and approves attendance, lesson notes, and result approvals within the approved primary scope.

### Principal

- Oversees the secondary section.
- Controls secondary academic structure and class/teacher assignment validity.

### Form Teacher

- Owns class-level coordination and attendance responsibilities for a class.
- Distinct from subject teaching.

### Subject Teacher / Specialist Teacher

- Assigned to subject/class combinations under approval.
- Can submit scores within authorization scope.

### Parent / Guardian

- Views linked children’s permitted information and approved records.
- Must be restricted to the same tenant.

### Student

- Accesses personal learning, assessment, and report data when allowed by policy.
- Access must be strictly scoped to self.
