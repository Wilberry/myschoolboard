# Announcements and Communication

## Communication surfaces

### School Notice Board

- School-wide official communication.
- Restricted to authorized administrators.
- Parents and students see according to policy.

### Class Announcements

- Scoped to authorized class or section.
- Teachers can only target their authorized authority.
- Parents do not see class announcements.

## Constraints

- These are separate channels and must not share one permission model.
- Notifications must be tenant-aware and audience-scoped.
