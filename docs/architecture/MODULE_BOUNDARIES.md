# Module Boundaries

## Platform operations

Responsible for SaaS operator functions such as tenant lifecycle, platform settings, subscriptions, support access, and operational monitoring.

## Identity and authorization

Responsible for user accounts, roles, permissions, class/subject scopes, access restrictions, and audit events.

## School configuration

Responsible for tenant branding, calendar, sessions, terms, sections, classes, subjects, grading configuration, and school policy settings.

## Academic operations

Responsible for admissions, enrollments, attendance, assessments, lesson plans, result sheets, report cards, and academic integrity.

## Finance

Responsible for fee structures, charges, payment evidence, verification, receipts, and reconciliation.

## Communication

Responsible for notices, announcements, notifications, and portal-specific communication.

## Retention and audit

Responsible for deletion lifecycle, recovery, retention, and audit visibility without violating tenant boundaries.

## Files and integrations

Responsible for storage, generated documents, integrated communication channels, and external payment or email providers.
