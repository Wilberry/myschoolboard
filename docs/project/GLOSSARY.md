# Glossary

## Core terms

- Tenant: one school or school group within the SaaS platform.
- Platform Super Admin: SaaS operator with platform-level authority only.
- School Super Admin: school-specific administrator with school-level authority.
- HM / Head of Primary: authority for the primary section within defined scope.
- Principal: authority for the secondary section within defined scope.
- Form Teacher: class-level coordination and attendance authority for a specific assigned class.
- Subject Teacher: teacher assigned to a subject across one or more classes.
- Section: academic division such as primary or secondary.
- Class: a school class within a section and session.
- Subject assignment: subject-class-teacher authorization record.
- Form Teacher assignment: class-level responsibility distinct from subject teaching.
- Academic session: annual school cycle.
- Term: subdivision within an academic session.
- Result sheet: class-level aggregation view for scores before final report-card publication.
- Published report card: final approved report authorized for parent or student visibility.
- School Trash: recoverable school-level deletion retention window.
- Platform Trash: retained deletion trail for platform operations and recovery.

## Common conventions

- All records are tenant-scoped unless explicitly defined as platform-level.
- Report cards are not considered official until final School Super Admin publication.
- Approval states must be explicit and auditable.
- School branding is configurable and not a hard-coded product identity.
